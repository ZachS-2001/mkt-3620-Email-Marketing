How we build the list: 3 customer groups

Every method below is permission-based. People opt in on purpose, know what they will receive, and get something useful for joining. That keeps our list healthy, our open rates high, and our spam complaints low.

Iowa State students and visiting families

Students who need a car for breaks, internships, or errands, and parents visiting on move-in, game, and graduation weekends.

Where and how we collect it

QR code posters in residence halls, the Memorial Union, and campus bulletin boards, plus tabling at move-in week and student org fairs. We also run organic Instagram and TikTok posts and a campus-targeted Instagram ad around game weekends and spring break.

Immediate value

15% off a first rental, delivered as an instant digital code after they confirm their email. Table visitors also get entered to win a free weekend rental.

Long-term promise

First access to booking for football weekends, graduation, and break travel, when cars sell out. Student-only deals and a referral bonus: one free rental day for each friend who rents.

Online visitors and trip planners

People searching for a rental in Ames, comparing prices, or starting a reservation online and leaving before they finish.

Where and how we collect it

The sign-up form at the top of our website, an exit-intent pop-up on the booking page, and a "save my quote" email option at checkout. We reach them through Google search ads for "car rental Ames" and Facebook retargeting.

Immediate value

The same 15% off first-rental code, plus a free downloadable "Ames and Central Iowa Road Trip Guide" for anyone who joins from a blog or social post.

Long-term promise

Holiday and weekend price drops before they are public, booking reminders when their dates approach, and helpful guides (winter driving, what to know before renting).

Local counter customers

Ames residents and business travelers who rent in person, including people whose own car is at a local body shop or dealership.

Where and how we collect it

A "Want deals by email?" opt-in line on the rental agreement tablet at the counter (unchecked by default), and a QR code on the keychain tag and on the receipt. Staff mention it in person. We also leave flyers at partner body shops and dealerships.

Immediate value

$10 off their next rental, emailed once they confirm. It is valid for 90 days, so it gives them a reason to come back.

Long-term promise

A loyalty program: every 5th rental earns a free upgrade. Subscribers also get VIP offers for returning customers and a heads-up about local events that fill up the lot, so they can book early.

Why this email follows best practices

  • Subject line: 6 words, plain, specific, no ALL CAPS or exclamation marks.
  • Personalized and readable as text: uses the subscriber's first name (with a "Hi there," fallback if blank). The offer, dates, and code are in plain text, so the message works with images turned off.
  • One clear call to action: a single button leading to one landing page that matches the email's promise.
  • Spam prevention: real sender name and address, a relevant subject, a reminder of why they are receiving it, a physical address, and an easy unsubscribe.
  • Growth link: "Forward to a friend" helps good subscribers bring in new ones.
